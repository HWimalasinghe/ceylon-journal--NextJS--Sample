- `hero.webp`: cinematic sunrise aerial scene inspired by Sigiriya, lush forest and mist, quiet luxury editorial aesthetic, wide composition with negative space on left.
- `tea.webp`: premium editorial scene of a tea estate bungalow in Sri Lanka's highlands, emerald terraced tea fields, hazy layered mountains and morning light.
- `wildlife.webp`: editorial scene of a wild Asian elephant walking in golden scrub at sunset, Sri Lankan national park inspiration.
- `coast.webp`: editorial scene of a secluded palm-fringed Sri Lankan beach with a boutique villa, turquoise sea and warm afternoon light.
